# Cercle de roches — standing stone model

The six menhirs in each healing circle share one bundled GLB model, used with
minor changes in rotation and scale. The model is 168 triangles and 24 KiB.

- Model: **Standing Stone (Andean Terraces and Stone Village)**
- Source: https://3dassets.dev/assets/andean-terraces-and-stone-village-standing-stone-5a08079e
- Original GLB: https://cdn.3dassets.dev/assets/38258/v1/model.glb
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain dedication)
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The GLB is bundled locally so the game does not depend on the asset CDN at runtime.
