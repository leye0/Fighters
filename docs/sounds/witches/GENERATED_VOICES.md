# Role-based game voices

English role voices generated with `qwen/qwen3-tts` on Replicate. 5 formation lines, 4 interaction lines, 4 attack lines for each of four roles (the fourth is reserved for a future character). All battle commands are independently designed forceful shouts; Witch `attack/*_02.mp3` uses the directly designed takes approved by the creator, not the calm-reference clones.

The replaced Freesound voice clips were removed (recoverable in Git history); retained `work/` and Orc `loud/` sounds retain their original attributions and licenses. Audio is pre-generated and served statically; `REPLICATE_API_KEY` is never included in the game.
